## What to Look For

Buying a quality timepiece is about the details you cannot see in a photo. Before you pay, check these five points:

- **Case finishing** — brushed sides, polished edges, no sharp corners
- **Dial printing** — crisp markers, aligned hands, no dust under the crystal
- **Bracelet feel** — solid links, firm clasp, smooth edges
- **Movement** — steady beat, correct date change, decent power reserve
- **After-sales** — a seller who answers, replaces and repairs

## 3 Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Buying on photos alone** — always ask for a video before shipping
2. **Ignoring the clasp** — the clasp is the first thing that fails on a cheap watch
3. **No after-sales plan** — a watch without support is a gamble

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Real video and photos before shipping
- [ ] Tracking number provided
- [ ] Water resistance tested before packing
- [ ] Repair and maintenance support confirmed
