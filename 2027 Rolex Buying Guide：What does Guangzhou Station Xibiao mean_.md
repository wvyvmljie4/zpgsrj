## What to Look For

The difference between a great watch and a regret is usually five small details. Go through this list before you pay:

- **Crown & Case** — the crown should thread smoothly and the case should feel dense, not hollow
- **Hands & Markers** — alignment and lume application tell you more than the spec sheet
- **Clasp & Links** — this is where cheap watches fail first
- **Crystal** — ask whether it is sapphire-coated and check for dust
- **Seller Support** — replies, repairs and resends are part of the price

## 3 Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trusting studio photos** — insist on a live video of the actual piece
2. **Skipping the movement check** — accuracy matters after the first week
3. **Buying without a tracking number** — no tracking means no accountability
